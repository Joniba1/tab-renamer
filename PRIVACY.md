# Tab Renamer privacy policy

_Last updated: October 23, 2026_

Tab Renamer is a browser extension that lets you give tabs custom titles. This policy explains what
it stores and what it doesn't do.

## What the extension stores

When you rename a tab, Tab Renamer saves:

- the page address (URL) or URL prefix the rename applies to;
- the title you entered;
- the time you last changed it.

This is saved with Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your computer only. It is
not synced to your Google account or to other devices.

## What the extension reads

On each page you open, the extension reads the page address to check whether you renamed it. If you
did, it sets the page's title to yours. It does not read the page's content, your browsing activity
on other pages, form data, or anything else.

## What leaves your computer

Nothing. Tab Renamer has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not
share, sell or transmit any data to anyone. The only file it ever creates is the backup you download
yourself with **Export**.

## Removing your data

Remove single renames, or use **Clear all**, on the extension's manage page. Uninstalling the
extension deletes everything it stored.
